# Plagiarism-Proof Notes Hub

Plagiarism-Proof Notes Hub is an ambitious multi-platform ecosystem that empowers students and teachers to create, transform, and share original study material. The platform blends OCR, AI rewriting, plagiarism detection, secure storage, and collaborative workflows to eliminate copying while accelerating learning.

## Vision
- **Always Original**: Automatically rewrite handwritten and digital notes into unique, meaning-preserving text.
- **AI-Assisted Learning**: Summaries, flashcards, and insights tailored to each learner.
- **Secure by Design**: End-to-end encryption, watermarking, and compliance with global regulations.
- **Collaborative**: Group editing, teacher portals, and version-controlled workspaces across every device.

## Technology Stack Highlights
- Cross-platform clients built with Kotlin Multiplatform, Compose, Tauri, and Next.js.
- AI services leveraging transformer models, semantic similarity scoring, and content safety filters.
- Event-driven microservices deployed on Kubernetes with GitOps automation and full observability.
